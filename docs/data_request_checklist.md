# Institutional Data Request Checklist

Before requesting records, agree on the prediction point and outcome period with the supervisor and authorized institutional staff.

## Minimum request

- At least three completed academic periods where possible
- A de-identified student key consistent across permitted data sources
- Programme, semester, and academic year
- Attendance known at the selected snapshot date
- Assessment marks known at the selected snapshot date
- Assignment completion known at the selected snapshot date
- Previous GPA and prior backlogs
- End-of-term failed-subject count, GPA, and withdrawal outcome
- Field definitions, GPA scale, missing-value codes, and policy changes

## Questions for the institution

1. At what week would intervention still be useful?
2. What outcome should represent a genuine need for academic support?
3. How many students can faculty realistically review each week?
4. Which interventions are actually available?
5. Which groups require fairness auditing, and is that use legally approved?
6. How long may research records and generated risk outputs be retained?
7. Who may view student-level explanations and intervention history?

## Do not request by default

- Student names, personal email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses
- Private messages, counselling notes, medical information, or free-text comments
- Religion, caste, ethnicity, disability, gender, or economic status without a documented and approved purpose
- Information recorded after the prediction date as a model feature

## Before transfer

- Obtain supervisor and institutional approval.
- Document the lawful/approved purpose and permitted users.
- Agree on secure transfer, storage, access control, retention, and deletion.
- Replace institutional student numbers with research identifiers.
- Confirm that re-identification keys remain with an authorized institutional custodian.
